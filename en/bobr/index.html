Beavers

Beavers — remarkable rodents and nature's engineers, creators of complex dams and lodges.

10 Amazing Facts:

  • 🌳Build dams up to 500 meters long
  • 🦷Teeth grow 0.5 cm per month
  • 🏠Lodges have underwater entrances for safety
  • 💦Can hold breath for 15 minutes
  • 🌍Restore ecosystems with their ponds
  • 👪Live in family groups up to 10 members
  • 📏1.3 m body length, 30 kg weight
  • 🍃Eat 1 ton of bark and branches yearly
  • 🕰️Lifespan: 10-15 years in wild
  • 🌍20% of global population lives in Russia
